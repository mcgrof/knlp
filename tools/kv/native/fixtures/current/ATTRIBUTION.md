# Wikipedia excerpt attribution

The frozen token arrays contain excerpts by Wikipedia contributors.
They are tokenized and windowed from the pinned English Wikipedia dataset;
no claim is made that they are unchanged complete articles. Article pages
link their contributor histories. The excerpts remain under the dataset
card’s CC BY-SA 3.0 / GFDL terms; the software MIT license does not replace
those source terms.
