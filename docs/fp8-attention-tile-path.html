FP8 KV attention · H100 tile path and pre-bias closure

The kernel family decides the speed. The final serving gate decides the default.

The earlier tile-path result stands: on one audited SM90 Tensor Core family, symmetric K8/V8 beats K16/V8 by 16–19% at bandwidth-bound shapes, while both lose at batch 1. That speed result does not make ordinary symmetric FP8 deployable for biased-key Qwen models, where key quantization destroys retrieval quality.

Bias-aware symmetric FP8 repairs that numerical failure and passes the served-geometry mutation suite, but it fails the frozen equal-memory serving gate. Pre-bias at concurrency 48 delivers 0.654× the completed-request rate of K16/V8 at concurrency 32 and 2.056× its p95 request latency. The pre-bias optimization line is closed; K16/V8 remains the serving default, and pre-bias remains only a documented capacity option.

KEEP THE BOUNDED RESULT

Bias-aware symmetric FP8 restores needle retrieval to 1.00 at 8K, 16K, and 30,720 tokens where ordinary K8/V8 reads 0.00, and it provides 1.5× the measured capacity of K16/V8. The 200-item GSM8K screen did not resolve a difference; it does not establish equivalence.

STOP THE KERNEL LINE

K16/V8 stays the default for biased-key models. Do not spend another optimization pass on this pre-bias kernel. Reopen only for materially new hardware, a different kernel family, or a new representation hypothesis.

Protocol note. The frozen review requested five interleaved serving repetitions; the final record contains two ABAB blocks. The countersign explicitly waives the remaining three for this negative engineering decision because the blocks agree, throughput is about 46% below the pass boundary, latency independently fails, and the common-concurrency sweep points the same way. This is not a five-repetition performance certification.

Apply the closed conclusions

LOCK: KERNEL FAMILY CHANGES THE SPEED ORDERING

Keep the exact kernel, JIT URI, cubin, layout, transform mode, and launch policy attached to every result. Storage dtype alone does not predict speed.

LOCK: SAME-FAMILY K8/V8 SPEED WIN

On the audited SM90 Tensor Core family, ordinary K8/V8 is 16–19% faster than K16/V8 at the bandwidth-bound shapes and loses at batch 1.

REJECT: ORDINARY K8/V8 FOR BIASED-KEY QWEN

The speed win is unusable where key bias makes symmetric quantization numerically destructive. Retrieval, not kernel timing, rejects this default.

LOCK: PRE-BIAS CORRECTNESS AND CAPACITY

The shipping level-9 correction passes the served mutation gate, restores long-context retrieval, and measures 1.5× K16/V8 capacity.

LOCK: K16/V8 IS THE SERVING DEFAULT

It preserves the quality rescue without the pre-bias kernel's decisive serving penalty.

REJECT: ANOTHER PRE-BIAS TUNING PASS

The frozen equal-memory gate fails at 0.654× throughput and 2.056× p95 latency. The optimization line is closed.

Campaign record and disposition

Phase 2 is no longer a plan. The same-family speed question, the biased-key quality failure, the corrective representation, and the final serving decision have all been measured.

Selected and audited the SM90 Tensor Core family.Exact dispatch, generated modules, resource use, correctness, and same-family dtype controls were retained with the artifacts.
Closed K8/V8 versus K16/V8 at the kernel level.Symmetric K8/V8 wins the bandwidth-bound same-family cells, demonstrating that the earlier CUDA-core conversion loss was not a storage-format law.
Separated speed from quality.Ordinary K8/V8 fails catastrophically on biased-key Qwen even when its kernel is fast. K16/V8 preserves quality by leaving keys unquantized.
Implemented bias-aware symmetric FP8.Pre-bias preserves the compact K8/V8 representation while correcting the key-bias failure and restoring long-context retrieval.
Passed the served mutation suite.Clean level 9 passes; injected live defects fail; the level-8-only offset defect is caught where expected.
Reproduced the kernel penalty.Five-process Latin-square runs at two geometries and two contexts confirm that pre-bias remains slower than K16 and that level 8 does not help.
Failed the equal-memory serving gate.At the measured capacity points, pre-bias@48 produces 0.654× K16/V8@32 completed requests per second and 2.056× its p95 latency.
Closed the line.Preserve the representation and artifacts as a capacity option; deploy K16/V8 by default; do not authorize another tuning pass.
No further campaign spend is authorized for this kernel line. A materially different hardware target, kernel family, or representation is new research and must begin with a new hypothesis and frozen gate.
