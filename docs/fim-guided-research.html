AI R&D · Fisher information

One signal.
Several research questions.

The diagonal Fisher Information Matrix (FIM) uses expected squared gradients as a sensitivity signal. These experiments explore how that signal can guide pruning, precision and placement, including estimates drawn from Adam’s second-moment state.
